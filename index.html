What I’m doing now

The day job

Business and data analyst at Capital One since 2023. Most of my week is SQL: building the pipelines that turn very large tables into something a team can act on, designing the tests that say whether it worked, and translating between the business side and the data scientists.

The night job

Part-time and online, I’m working through Penn Engineering’s MSE in Data Science. Three units (four courses) down, seven to go; done in 2028 if I keep the pace. It is slower than a full-time program and considerably harder to explain at parties (people tend to look at me like I’m insane).

Transit

Systems, ranked

Unsurprisingly, I am also a massive fan of public transit. Whenever I go on a trip, I plan around the local transit map. Even just an afternoon riding a system end to end feels like a day well-spent to me. You learn a lot about what a city thinks of its people by how it moves them. Here's my ranking of my personal top 12:

01 J Z MTANew York: I rely on it every day. More sentimental than operational.
